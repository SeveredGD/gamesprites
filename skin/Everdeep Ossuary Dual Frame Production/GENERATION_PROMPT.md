# Regeneration prompt

Built-in ImageGen was used in precise-object-edit mode with two inputs:

1. the selected Ossuary Shrine Cabinet concept as the geometry/design target;
2. the current ornate-silver grimoire composite as the authoritative color, material, contrast, and highlight reference.

The edit requested faithful preservation of the twin rectangular wells, narrow funerary divider, skull/bone flourishes, chains, straight orthographic silhouette, and long uniform rails. It changed the material treatment to cool desaturated aged silver highlights over charcoal-black recesses with only restrained warm grime. It also reserved the middle 55–65% of every long rail as a stretch-safe region, kept elaborate details in fixed caps, requested a vertically repeatable divider middle, prohibited books/perspective/text, and requested transparent exterior and content wells.

Because the returned PNG encoded the transparency preview as RGB, `build_ossuary_slices.py` converted the exterior and both known content wells to real alpha before exporting the production masters and slices.

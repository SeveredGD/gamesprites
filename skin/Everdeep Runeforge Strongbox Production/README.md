# Runeforge Strongbox Dual Frame — Production

Production preparation of `02-runeforge-strongbox.png`, matching the deliverable structure of the Ossuary dual-frame package.

## Use these files

- `runeforge-strongbox-master-1284x540.png` — fixed-size drop-in master matching the Ossuary production stage.
- `runeforge-strongbox-master-1935x813.png` — full-resolution transparent assembled master.
- `runeforge-strongbox-source-master-1672x941.png` — transparent master at the original concept dimensions.
- `slices/` — eight outer-frame pieces plus a five-piece independent divider.
- `slice-manifest.json` — content wells and assembly rules.
- `runeforge-strongbox-alpha-preview.jpg` — dark-background transparency check.
- `runeforge-strongbox-slice-guide.png` — visual assembly map.

The divider is deliberately five-sliced. Its top and bottom caps and central rune medallion remain fixed; only the quiet upper and lower rails stretch vertically. This avoids deforming the most important Runeforge ornament.

For the current fixed equipment/vault stage, use the 1284×540 master. For resizable layouts, assemble the exported slices according to the manifest.

# Journal update required

The glove slot and its mechanics must be documented in the in-game Journal during the same release. Do not ship the slot with only tooltip explanations.

## Suggested Journal entry

### Gloves

Gloves are an equipment slot focused on direct-hit effects, attack speed, ailments, and recovery. They can drop wherever other ordinary equipment drops.

Every glove affix has a tier. T1 is the lowest range and T3 is the highest. Any glove can roll any tier, but stronger affix tiers become more common in later difficulties.

**Life on Hit** restores a percentage of maximum Life after a direct attack deals damage. **Energy Shield on Hit** restores part of your existing maximum Energy Shield. A normal glove can have only one of these recovery affixes. Some unique gloves can have both.

**Crushing Blow** removes part of the target's current Life before the attack deals its normal damage. It is less effective against elites and bosses.

**Open Wounds** prevents the target from healing and deals physical damage over four seconds. Reapplying it normally refreshes the stronger wound instead of stacking it.

**Culling Strike** kills non-boss enemies below the listed Life threshold. Guardians use half the threshold. Bosses cannot be culled and instead take additional direct-hit damage while below the threshold.

**Stagger on Hit** delays the target's next attack. The delay is reduced against elites and bosses.

Only the first damaging hit from an attack can trigger glove effects. Additional projectiles, AoE targets, echoes, damage over time, companions, thorns, and reflected damage do not trigger them again.
